# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-wrench" %}Projects

Our lab is broadly interested in understanding the mechanisms that drive genome innovation across the tree of life. We are particularly fascinated by the role of mobile DNA in shaping genome structure, adaptation, and biodiversity across deep evolutionary time. By combining comparative genomics, evolutionary theory, and computational biology, we seek to understand how mobile DNA influences genome evolution and host adaptation across the tree of life. We are also passionate about open science and reproducible research, and we actively develop and use open-source tools to facilitate collaborative research and transparent scientific communication. Finally, we are committed to training the next generation of scientists and fostering a collaborative and inclusive research environment, where all members of our lab can thrive and contribute to our shared goals of understanding genome ecology and biodiversity change.
