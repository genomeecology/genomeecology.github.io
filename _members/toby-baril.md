Toby is a [DDLS](https://www.scilifelab.se/data-driven/) fellow and group leader at the Swedish Museum of Natural History. He has a broad range of interests spanning transposable elements (TEs), genomics from populations to entire kingdoms, and the development of tools to facilitate genomic research. His research focuses on understanding the mechanisms that drive genome innovation and host adaptation across the tree of life, with a particular emphasis on the role of mobile DNA in shaping genome structure, adaptation, and biodiversity over deep evolutionary time.

Toby's major aims include leveraging emerging technologies, such an interpretable AI, to explore the diversity of mobile DNA across the tree of life, developing computational tools to analyze genomic data, and investigating the evolutionary dynamics of TEs in various organisms. By combining comparative genomics, evolutionary theory, and computational biology, he seeks to uncover the intricate relationships between mobile genetic elements and their host genomes, and to understand how these interactions contribute to the evolution of biodiversity.
