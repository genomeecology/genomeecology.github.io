Toby is a group leader and [DDLS](https://www.scilifelab.se/data-driven/) fellow at the Swedish Museum of Natural History. He has a broad range of interests spanning transposable elements (TEs), genomics from populations to entire kingdoms, and the development of tools to facilitate genomic research. His research focuses on understanding the mechanisms that drive genome innovation and host adaptation across the tree of life, with a particular emphasis on the role of mobile DNA in shaping genome structure, adaptation, and biodiversity over deep evolutionary time. Toby's major aims include leveraging emerging technologies, such an interpretable AI, to explore the diversity of mobile DNA across the tree of life, developing computational tools to analyze genomic data, and investigating the evolutionary dynamics of TEs in various organisms. 

Outside the lab, Toby spends most of his time exploring the outdoors, whether it's climbing, trail running, cycling, skiing, wild swimming, or just wandering around in the woods. He is a strong advocate for Type 2 fun, which balances well with the desk work. Toby is also passionate about third-wave speciality coffee, and will preach about the virtues of a good pour-over or espresso to anyone who will listen.
