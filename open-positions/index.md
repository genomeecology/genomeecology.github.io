# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-microscope" %}Open Positions

We are building an inclusive and collaborative research environment and are always looking for talented, curious, and motivated people to join us.

Our research sits at the intersection of evolutionary genomics, biodiversity science, and computational biology. We welcome applicants from a wide range of backgrounds. A genuine enthusiasm for evolutionary genomics, biodiversity research, and open science is essential, and experience with scripting or programming is highly valued, although we are equally excited to support people who are keen to develop these skills.

If you are interested in joining the lab, please apply to one of our advertised positions or [reach out to Toby directly](/contact/) to discuss potential opportunities. Positions will always be advertised officially through the NRM website, which you can access via the links below. Toby is also happy to discuss fellowship applications and independent funding schemes, including programmes such as DDLS, Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions, EMBO, Carl Tryggers, and Wenner-Gren, among others.

Check out the full ad by clicking on the job header.
