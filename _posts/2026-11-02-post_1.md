Toby has joined the [Naturhistoriska Riksmuseet (Swedish Museum of Natural History)](https://www.nrm.se/engelska/in-english) in Stockholm, Sweden as a [DDLS Fellow](https://www.ddls.se/). He will be working on understanding the mechanisms shaping genome structure, adaptation, and biodiversity across the tree of life. Check out our [open positions](positions) if you are interested in joining the lab!
